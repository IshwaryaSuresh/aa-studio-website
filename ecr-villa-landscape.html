ECR Villa Landscape

Location

East Coast Road, Tamil Nadu

Type

Residential garden

Scope

Landscape Architecture

An outdoor living landscape by the coast.

The brief was simple to say and rich to resolve: contemporary biophilic outdoor living. A coastal garden on the East Coast Road that feels lush and tactile, built from sculpted greens, stone and timber and warmed by ambient light. AA+ carried the idea through two complete design options, each moving from a concept mood board to a full master plan, program zones and a detailed planting sheet.

Both schemes are drawn as a journey rather than a single lawn. An arrival sequence gives way to quiet retreat corners, a family play trail and a reading nook with a swing, while water and fire anchor the garden as focal moments. Option 1 leans into tropical softness, with stepping paths, intimate lounge pockets, a zen-style water court and a sunken fire-lounge. Option 2 sets a more sensory, structured language of curved mosaic paving, vine and floral arches, meandering bioswales and a reflexology stone garden.

At the social heart of the plan sits a dining pocket: a long communal timber table under a vine-covered pergola, paired with a brick pizza oven and a built-in barbecue counter, lit by warm lanterns and string lights over stone paving.

The planting is native-first. A shortlist drawn from the villa's own planting sheet leads with Ixora and Bambusa for flowering hedges and screening clusters, bird's-nest fern for shaded pockets, and curtain creeper as a cascading green layer along the pool deck and edges. The material palette runs warm sand, forest green, water slate and amber glow, carried in aged timber, terracotta and wicker.
